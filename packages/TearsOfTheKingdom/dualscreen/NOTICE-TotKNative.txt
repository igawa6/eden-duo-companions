This package's Tears of the Kingdom reader (native/modules/totk_gamedata.h and
0100F2C0115B6000.cpp, packages/TearsOfTheKingdom) ports game-memory layout knowledge and flag names from
TotK Native (https://github.com/AurelioB/totk-android), games/totk/runtime/companion_snapshot.c and
companion.h: the GameData table offsets/strides and Vector3 value offset, MurmurHash3 flag names
("PlayerStatus.*", "Pouch.*", "MapData.*"), string-array elements, the pouch partition selector, the
category/slot tables, and the scene -> resident actor "Player" walk (actor +0x218/+0x2B4/+0x2C0).
