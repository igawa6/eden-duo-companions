# Xenoblade Chronicles X: Definitive Edition companion

Title `0100453019AA8000`, game version 1.0.2 (update v131072, main build
`2A720C7CE5C84905C1C6847211F670FBB31842B6`). Requires Eden Duo 1.4.1 or newer (runtime 25). The
module refuses other builds; the companion then shows a "Companion not available" notice and reads
nothing. Current package: `1.0.0`.

The bottom screen has five tabs:

- **Map.** The FrontierNav map of Mira with its hex grid, NLA and the BLADE Barracks, in two modes:
  **Overworld** (the whole planet) and **Area** (the district around you). Your position is the
  game's own arrow and turns with you; a parked Skell shows its own icon. Drag to pan, pinch to zoom,
  Find Self to come back. Continents you have not visited stay dark. The right pane lists your party
  with HP (and TP before you own a Skell) and the controlled member's Skell with fuel and HP. Tap a
  member to make them the controlled character.
- **Party.** The four party members with HP and TP and the controlled member marked. Pick a member
  for a profile card: **Overview** (class, Lv., Rank, HP, TP, the stats table with all six
  resistances, their Skell) and **Gear** (the seven ground gear slots with Lv. and Additional
  Effects, scrolling). **Set as Controlled Character** switches control.
- **Skell.** The controlled member's Skell drawn in its own paint with its equipped weapons, its
  name, fuel, HP, GP and Skell Insurance.
- **Battle.** Opens by itself when any party member enters a fight and closes a little after the
  fight ends. Party cards with HP, TP, GP while mounted, Down and the controlled member, the
  controlled member's Skell with HP, GP, fuel and insurance, Set as Controlled Character and
  **Battle Commands**.
- **Settings.** Hide the minimap and the Skell fuel gauge on the top screen, Reduced motion, and the
  game and companion versions. Touch feedback follows the Android system setting.

Every page has the **Skell** button: Board or Exit when your Skell is close, **Return to Skell**
(hold) when it is far away. On the title screen the companion shows the game's logo and "No save
loaded"; while the game loads, the last page is dimmed with a loading plate; when a game menu is
open the page dims and says "Menu open", and game actions wait until it closes.

## This companion changes the game

It does more than read. While it runs:

- **Top HUD hide.** The minimap and the Skell fuel gauge are hidden on the top screen by default,
  through the runtime's guarded memory writes (`write_batch`). Only the game's live HUD state
  changes; your saved Display Settings are never touched. Both toggles are in Settings, and the
  HUD comes back when you switch them off or close the companion.
- **Helper actions.** A small in-game helper (format-2 load plan) calls the game's own functions to
  **Board or Exit your Skell**, **switch the controlled character** and **Return to Skell**. They
  run only when the game itself would allow them (on the field, no menu, no event, not in flight)
  and are refused with "Not available now" otherwise.
- **Battle Commands** presses the game's **+** button for you, which opens the game's own battle
  menu. It is offered only while the field takes input.

Physical controls stay available. Haptics follow the Android system setting; Reduced motion turns
off the page fades and the press flash.

The package ships no game art. Map pictures, UI frames and icons, fonts, portraits, names, stats and
the Skell model and paint are read from your own game files (`sts.arh` / `sts.ard`) and your save
data at run time.

## Rebuilding

```sh
# Needs your own dumped 1.0.2 main NSO, clang and LLVM tools:
python3 tools/xcx/build_helper_plan.py --nso /path/to/exefs/main --output <load-plan.json>
cmake -S native/modules -B build-xcx -G Ninja -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release -DEDEN_SOURCE_ROOT=/path/to/eden-duo -DBUILD_TESTING=ON
cmake --build build-xcx --target dsmod-xcx
cmake --build build-xcx
ctest --test-dir build-xcx -R xcx --output-on-failure
llvm-strip --strip-unneeded build-xcx/0100453019AA8000.so
python3 tools/build_dualscreen_package.py --package packages/XenobladeChroniclesXDefinitiveEdition --output dist \
  --title-id 0100453019AA8000 --version 1.0.0 --min-runtime 25 \
  --module linux-x86_64=build-xcx/0100453019AA8000.so \
  --build-id 2A720C7CE5C84905C1C6847211F670FBB31842B6000000000000000000000000
```

Android modules use the NDK CMake toolchain with `ANDROID_ABI=arm64-v8a`; pass the result as an
extra `--module android-arm64-v8a=<path>`. The opt-in `xcx-preview` and `xcx-model-probe` tools
need your own game files and save; none of those inputs are packaged.
