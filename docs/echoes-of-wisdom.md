# Echoes of Wisdom companion

Development companion for Eden Duo runtime 22, targeting AYN Thor with a 1240 × 1080 bottom screen. Supports game update 1.1.0, build `9F221E1AFA65BA5443E955158AB97DA871E5E41F000000000000000000000000`. Other builds are refused.

Hold Right on the physical D-pad during ordinary gameplay to open the native Echo selector and bottom-screen grid. Scroll and tap an owned Echo to steer the native highlight. Keep holding until selection settles, then release D-pad to select it. The companion never casts an Echo. Release or a changed native selector cancels pending steering.

The held selector shows a full-width six-column, three-row grid. Swipe the grid to scroll through learned Echoes. Tap a connected tab at the top left to choose Last Used, Most Used, Last Learned, Cost or Type directly. The gold tab follows the confirmed native sort; tapping the current mode does nothing. The counter is right-aligned at the top right. Favorites are managed in Notebook. The temporary held grid restores the previous local page and inspected entry.

Notebook, Items and Equipment share a five-column collection grid and a narrower detail pane. Names wrap and descriptions scroll separately. Notebook has a favorite toggle and Equip button. Equipment offers Equip or Remove where allowed by the native game, including accessory capacity. Outfit changes start from gameplay because their native commit requires closing the pause menu. Edible ingredients offer Eat or Drink; other items have no invented action. These buttons steer the original game controls and wait for native readback. The game may briefly show its own selector or pause menu during an action. The companion never casts an Echo or directly writes inventory state.

Artwork, localized messages and original fonts are read from the player's own game/session. None are distributed in the package. Unsupported font/locale combinations remain unavailable.

Map is the home page. Its framed board uses three quarters of the screen width and the height above the footer, with a left-aligned area-name heading. Original terrain proportions are preserved; drag and pinch pan and zoom. A right-hand rail uses the game’s HUD discs: X for Bind, Y for the current Echo, and D-pad Up for Swordfighter Form. Tap the Y disc to inspect that Echo in Notebook. X and Up are informational; the companion does not activate those abilities. The Swordfighter disc currently appears after ten learned Echoes as an availability heuristic, rather than from a verified ownership reader.

The taller slate footer uses larger bold cream labels with black outlines and borders. Map has three footer buttons: Equipment, Items and Notebook. Each collection page has one wide Back button returning to Map. The native button artwork meets the bottom edge, following the Link’s Awakening companion’s navigation structure. Notebook, the held grid and the current-Echo disc show native cost triangles when current reduction state is verified; a grey final triangle represents a cost reduction.

Native story progress and weather decide whether the corresponding covered regions can appear. Zelda's original map icon marks supported Field locations and unambiguous authored location aliases. It hides for unknown projections, covered terrain, unavailable view readback or clipping at the viewport edge. Native place names keep a constant readable text size while zooming and use their authored map positions and stay hidden until all linked exploration regions are discovered. Overlapping names are suppressed. This conservative visibility rule may reveal a name later than the native game. Active rifts require current native story flags and remain clipped to revealed terrain. Individual rift names are not invented; the native rift marker metadata has no name labels. Collectible markers and maps inside small rifts remain unavailable.

The eight major dungeons use their original room artwork, floor definitions and saved exploration. Choose a floor in the right pane; drag and pinch the room map. Floors share one coordinate frame, preserving room scale and alignment when switching floors. Visited rooms use the native revealed artwork. Unvisited rooms appear only when the dungeon map item allows them, and unresolved conditional rooms stay hidden. Dungeon maps do not borrow Field terrain. Precise player markers and connecting paths are not yet shown. Native dungeon assets, floor definitions and exploration rules are tested; live dungeon entry and floor touch still require a suitable checkpoint.

Open the waypoint button beside the pin button to browse quick travel. Tap a revealed, unlocked waypoint, then Warp in the right pane. The companion opens and steers the original map, checks its Warp/Cancel choice and waits for arrival. Back cancels browsing. This development route supports Field destinations; interior and rift destinations remain unavailable. Unavailable state before confirmation or a changed save cancels the request without replay. After confirmation, native loading is allowed within a bounded arrival wait; success requires the gameplay HUD and verified destination. Avoid simultaneous physical inputs while the original travel menu is being steered.

Open the map-pin button in the area heading to choose a flag, heart, chest or diamond, then tap revealed terrain to place it. The picker opens in the right pane. With the picker open, tapping near an existing pin removes it. Back closes the tray. Up to24 companion-local pins persist in the emulator profile, across saves and sessions; they do not change the game's own map or save. A pin on unexplored terrain is hidden. Restart the game once after installing this development update so the host initializes the new persistent pin flags. Corrupt or unavailable stored pin data disables annotations rather than overwriting it with defaults.

During the verified opening Link scene, the bottom shows an empty map-style board with the original game logo and the native area title, Suthorn Ruins. Three staggered circles show Bow/X, Sword/Y and Bomb/A using the native button glyphs. Tapping a circle sends its matching native button while the opening HUD is authenticated. This opening view has no footer tabs. Zelda's Echo/Bind/Swordfighter rail and collection actions are hidden. This identifies a qualified opening scene, not the controlled character everywhere. Temporary unknown context hides the map. Ordinary pages also hide in unsupported contexts, including the currently unclassified Dojo/indoor fixture. After 64 uncertain source transitions a companion reload is required; old terrain is not reused across those scopes.

Current development limits: desktop native selection, inspection and the supported Field map have been exercised with real early/completed save fixtures. The marker requires runtime pan/zoom readback; older development binaries may hide it. Physical Thor touch comfort, Android/NCE behavior and performance remain unverified. Android library compilation alone is not a device pass. Native input movement matching the companion's expected movement cannot be distinguished from its own step.

The manifest is generated by `tools/echoes-of-wisdom/gen_manifest.py`. Native helper/test targets are in `native/modules/CMakeLists.txt`; build `dsmod-echoes` for the module. Original game assets, private saves, firmware and keys are not source or package inputs to distribute.
